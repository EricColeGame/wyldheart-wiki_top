If you're running a spell-heavy build in Mandragora: Whispers of the Witch Tree, Wyldheart crafting should sit near the top of your mid-game checklist. The relic occupies your off-hand slot, yet it behaves like a pure damage item: a 25% spell buff, a bonus to root chance, and a signature ability called Discharge. It weighs only 1.25, so it slides into almost any loadout without forcing you to rebuild your equipment around it. The reason Wyldheart crafting matters is timing — the item becomes available early enough to shape your power spike, and strong enough to stay equipped deep into late content.

This guide covers the exact recipe, the diagram's hiding spot near the Old Elevator Witch Stone, the full stat line, and practical advice for gathering every material without wasting hours. Whether you're a first-time player or returning after a patch, the process is the same: find the diagram, bank the goods, and hand everything to the Occultist.

## Wyldheart Stats at a Glance

Before you commit resources, it helps to know exactly what you're buying. The Wyldheart is classified as a Wyld Relic and an off-hand item, which means it competes directly with shields, tomes, and utility trinkets for that slot.

| Property | Value | What It Means in Practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Item Type | Wyld Relic (Off-Hand) | Equips in the off-hand slot |
| Spell Buff | 25% | A flat boost to your spell output |
| Weight | 1.25 | Very light; barely touches equipment burden |
| Ability | Discharge | A unique active tied to the relic |
| Extra Effect | +7% Root Chance | Adds a chance to lock enemies in place |
| Flavor Text | "Pulses with the heartbeat of the forest." | Lore flavor only |

The 25% spell buff is the headline number. Off-hand items in most action RPGs lean defensive, so a slot that pushes your magic damage upward is genuinely rare. The +7% root chance is the quieter winner: rooting an enemy interrupts movement, creates breathing room, and stacks naturally with any other root or immobilize source in your kit. At 1.25 weight, the relic is light enough that even burden-conscious players can carry it without a second thought.

One honest caveat — the reference material confirms that Discharge is the relic's listed ability but doesn't break down its damage numbers or cooldown. Treat any precise figures you see floating around as unverified until you test them yourself.

## The Complete Wyldheart Crafting Recipe

Crafting happens at the Occultist. You cannot simply buy the relic, and you cannot craft it without first learning the diagram — more on that in the next section. Here is the full shopping list.

| Material | Quantity | Typical Source | Farming Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wood | 5 | Resource nodes, crates, common enemies | Low — you likely already have it |
| Anima Core | 4 | Elite and boss encounters, rare pickups | High — the real bottleneck |
| Claw | 3 | Beast-type enemies | Medium |
| Flawed Emerald | 2 | Gem nodes, chests, hidden stashes | Medium |
| Gold | 800 | Selling loot, quest rewards, exploration | Medium-High early on |

The pattern here is telling. Wood and Claw are the kind of materials that pile up passively while you play, so you probably won't need a dedicated farming session for them. Anima Core is the opposite: it's the material that gates your progress, and four of them is a meaningful ask for a mid-game player. Flawed Emerald sits in the middle — common enough to find, but easy to sell by accident if you're not paying attention.

## Where to Find the Wyldheart Diagram

Without the diagram, the recipe will not appear at the Occultist no matter how many materials you're carrying. This is the single most common reason players get stuck, and the good news is that the location is fixed rather than random.

| Step | Action | Landmark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Travel to the Old Elevator Witch Stone | Old Elevator Witch Stone |
| 2 | Ride the elevator upward | The elevator itself |
| 3 | Head left from the top of the ride | Open path to the left |
| 4 | Cross the water to the building on the far side | Building across the water |
| 5 | Open the golden chest inside | Guarded golden chest |

Two details are worth emphasizing. First, the chest is guarded, so expect a fight before you can loot it — clear the area rather than trying to sprint past, because the interaction window can be interrupted. Second, the diagram is a one-time unlock. Once you've picked it up, the recipe stays available at the Occultist permanently, so there's no reason to delay grabbing it.

If you've already explored that area and don't remember seeing a golden chest, backtrack to the Witch Stone and re-walk the route slowly. It's easy to drift past the correct left-hand turn on a first pass.

## Wyldheart Crafting Cost: Who Should Actually Build It

The recipe isn't expensive in gold terms for a late-game character, but 800 Gold is a real commitment if you're still in the early hours. Here's how the relic lines up against common playstyles.

| Playstyle | Fit | Reasoning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spell-focused caster | Excellent | The 25% spell buff scales your primary damage source |
| Root / crowd-control build | Strong | +7% root chance layers onto existing immobilize effects |
| Hybrid melee-caster | Good | Light weight means no meaningful tradeoff |
| Pure melee or tank | Situational | The spell buff does little for a build that never casts |
| Low-weight / mobility build | Great | 1.25 weight is close to negligible |

The verdict is straightforward. If your damage comes from spells, Wyldheart crafting is close to a no-brainer — few off-hand options give you a direct percentage increase to spell output while also adding crowd control. If you never cast, the relic is a stat stick you'd be better off skipping in favor of a shield.

There's also an opportunity-cost angle worth considering. That 800 Gold could go toward upgrades, consumables, or other recipes. The way to think about it: does the relic replace something you'd otherwise buy? For most casters, the answer is yes.

## Farming Tips for Wyldheart Crafting Materials

Once you have the diagram, the grind becomes a shopping trip. These habits shorten it considerably.

| Habit | Why It Pays Off |
| --- | --- |
| Re-run elite encounters on a fixed route | Anima Cores are the bottleneck; repetition beats wandering |
| Break every crate, pile, and destructible | Wood and Gold accumulate passively |
| Check vendors before selling gems | Flawed Emeralds are recipe ingredients, not just vendor trash |
| Bank Gold before hunting the diagram | Avoid spending your 800 Gold on impulse upgrades |
| Clear guarded chest areas fully | Prevents interrupted interactions and missed loot |

A few extra pointers from player experience:

- **Do the diagram first.** Materials sitting in your inventory do nothing until the recipe is unlocked. Reversing the order costs you nothing but time.
- **Track your Anima Cores.** Since you need four, note how many you're holding before you start a session — it's the number that decides whether you're close or far.
- **Don't dismantle blindly.** If your inventory is full and you're purging, gems and cores are the last things that should go.
- **Weigh the slot, not just the stats.** If you're already carrying a heavy weapon set, the 1.25 weight makes this an easy addition rather than a burden calculation.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Most frustration around this relic traces back to a handful of avoidable errors.

- **Hunting materials before the diagram.** You'll finish the grind and still see nothing at the Occultist.
- **Assuming the chest is free loot.** It's guarded for a reason.
- **Selling Flawed Emeralds early.** Two of them are required, and they're easy to offload without thinking.
- **Crafting on a build that can't use it.** A pure melee character gets almost nothing from a spell buff.
- **Ignoring the root chance.** Players often treat +7% as filler, but in practice it's the stat that makes the relic feel good in extended fights.

## FAQ

**What is the exact Wyldheart crafting recipe?**

The recipe requires 5 Wood, 4 Anima Core, 3 Claw, 2 Flawed Emerald, and 800 Gold, and it must be crafted at the Occultist. You also need the Wyldheart diagram unlocked before the recipe appears.

**Where do I find the Wyldheart diagram?**

Start at the Old Elevator Witch Stone, ride the elevator up, head left, and cross the water to the building on the far side. The diagram sits inside a guarded golden chest within that building.

**Is Wyldheart crafting worth it for melee builds?**

Usually not. The relic's value comes almost entirely from its 25% spell buff and bonus root chance, so a character that relies on physical attacks gets little from it. A shield or defensive off-hand is the better call.

**Does the Wyldheart weigh down my loadout?**

Not meaningfully. At 1.25 weight, it's one of the lighter off-hand options available, which makes it a comfortable fit even for burden-sensitive builds.

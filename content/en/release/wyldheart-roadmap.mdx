## Wyldheart Roadmap at a Glance

Wyldheart is a co-op action RPG set in a rustic fantasy world where you survive the wilderness, craft your own gear, and push into dangerous dungeons either alone or with friends. That premise shapes the roadmap: the developers need working online co-op before anything else matters, which is why the beta and Early Access phases are structured around systems testing rather than content dumps.

Here is the confirmed shape of the Wyldheart roadmap as it stands today.

| Phase | Timing | Who gets in | What it represents |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kickstarter campaign | 2026 | Backers | Funding, beta tier sales, public roadmap reveal |
| Closed beta | From May 15, 2026 | Beta Access backers | First hands-on test of co-op and combat systems |
| Public demo | 2026 (shown ahead of PAX East 2026) | Everyone | A vertical slice that proves the core loop works |
| Early Access | Before the end of 2026 | PC players via Steam | The game's first purchasable, playable build |
| Full release (1.0) | Sometime in 2027 | Everyone | The finished campaign and all planned systems |

Three things stand out when you read that timeline carefully:

- **The gap between Early Access and 1.0 is short.** A launch window measured in months, not years, suggests the team intends to finish rather than rebuild.
- **The beta comes first, and it is closed.** Only backers with a Beta Access tier got in when it opened in May 2026, which keeps feedback focused.
- **Nothing after 1.0 has been promised.** Any post-launch roadmap is speculation until the studio says otherwise.

## Closed Beta: The First Playable Checkpoint

The closed beta opened on May 15, 2026, and it is the phase that matters most for the rest of the Wyldheart roadmap. Betas exist to catch problems that internal testing misses, and for a co-op RPG the biggest risks are almost always network-related rather than content-related.

Community reports from beta participants have focused on how the game handles a persistent campaign structure — the idea that your world and your friends' progress carry forward between sessions, similar to how a tabletop campaign works. That is a much harder technical problem than a simple lobby-based co-op mode, and it explains why the beta window is being used to stress-test sessions rather than to show off new regions.

| Beta focus area | What is being tested | Why it matters for later phases |
|---|---|---|
| Co-op networking | Hosting, joining, session persistence | A broken drop-in flow would sink the Early Access launch |
| Combat feel | Sword, axe, mace, bow, and staff techniques | Five weapon families need distinct weight and timing |
| Progression pacing | Early crafting and gear loops | Pacing fixes are far cheaper before Early Access |
| World tone | Rustic fantasy art direction and quest voice | Sets expectations for the full campaign |

If you are in the beta, the most useful thing you can do is report the boring problems — desyncs, stuck quest flags, inventory weirdness. Those are the issues that delay a roadmap.

## Early Access: What Arrives Before the End of 2026

The studio's stated plan is for Early Access to be out before the end of 2026. That is the firmest commitment on the Wyldheart roadmap, and it is worth understanding what Early Access means in practice for a game like this: you get a playable, purchasable build that is feature-complete in its core systems but still light on content and polish.

Expect the Early Access build to center on the loops that define Wyldheart — exploring the wilderness, surviving hostile environments, crafting gear from what you gather, and running dungeons with a small group. What you should not expect is a finished campaign. The scope of Early Access content has not been detailed publicly, and studios generally hold that information back until launch week so they can cut or add without backlash.

| Feature | Early Access (before end of 2026) | Full release 1.0 (2027) |
|---|---|---|
| Core campaign | Playable, incomplete | Complete |
| Weapon technique trees | Sword, axe, mace, bow, staff — live but evolving | Rebalanced and expanded |
| Co-op | Online play with friends | Polished matchmaking and stability |
| Crafting and survival | Core gear and gathering loops | Full recipe and biome coverage |
| Performance | Iterative patches | Launch-ready optimization |
| Platforms | PC via Steam | Additional platforms only if announced |

One practical tip: if you plan to play co-op with a fixed group, start together in Early Access rather than waiting. Persistent campaign progress is far less painful to manage when everyone levels at the same pace.

## Full Release 1.0: The 2027 Target

Wayfinder Studios has said it hopes to finish the Early Access period and ship the 1.0 build at some point in 2027. Note the phrasing — "sometime in 2027" is a window, not a date, and it is the correct way to read any entry on the Wyldheart roadmap that far out.

Three things have to go right for that window to hold.

| Readiness factor | Why it could shift the 1.0 date | What to watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Co-op stability | Network bugs scale with player count and geography | Patch notes that mention session fixes |
| Content volume | The campaign needs enough regions and dungeons to justify 1.0 | Early Access content drops |
| Balance and progression | Five weapon families plus crafting is a lot of surface area | Frequency of balance patches |

The good news is that the studio has been candid about the 2027 target instead of locking a specific day it cannot keep. For a small team shipping an online RPG, a loose window is a sign of realism rather than disorganization. If you want to judge progress for yourself, look at how often Early Access patches arrive and whether each one adds content or only fixes bugs — a healthy roadmap alternates between the two.

## FAQ

**When does Wyldheart leave Early Access?**
The current plan is for the 1.0 build to arrive sometime in 2027, after Early Access launches before the end of 2026. Those are the two anchors of the Wyldheart roadmap, and both remain windows rather than fixed dates.

**How do I get into the Wyldheart beta?**
Closed beta access was offered through the Kickstarter campaign as a dedicated Beta Access tier, with the beta opening on May 15, 2026. If you missed the campaign, watch official channels for any future public testing phases.

**Is Wyldheart coming to consoles?**
PC via Steam is the only platform confirmed so far. The developers have not announced console versions, so treat any console talk as speculation until it comes from the studio.

**Will the roadmap change?**
Almost certainly, and that is normal. Dates for online co-op RPGs move when networking or balance problems surface. The phases — beta, Early Access, 1.0 — are far more reliable than the specific months attached to them.

The scheduling angle is the standout. Wayfinder Studios has explicitly framed Wyldheart as a game for busy adults — campaigns are designed to be finished, not abandoned at hour forty. There's also the setting. Wyldheart leans into mythic fantasy with Fey folklore rather than the usual grimdark template, and players choose from three ancestries — Freefolk, Grimhorn, and Mossling — plus 19 backgrounds. A short, strange, character-driven campaign is a good fit for a console audience that already has plenty of sprawling open-world RPGs competing for its time.

That's a lot of inputs. A console version would need a genuinely redesigned radial or modifier system, not a keyboard mapping bolted onto a gamepad.
